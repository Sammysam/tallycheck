In short

For a small or medium company, the core books are covered as well as QuickBooks covers them: sales, purchases, payments, taxes, foreign currency, bank reconciliation and the main reports. This is especially true for companies in Sri Lanka.

Our app's advantages: it works offline, has no subscription, keeps the data on the company's own PC, has Sri Lankan payroll and withholding tax built in, stores HR documents encrypted, makes encrypted backups and allows unlimited users.

QuickBooks' advantages: automation (bank feeds, recurring transactions, reminders), inventory items, quotes and purchase orders, classes and projects, budgets, a custom report builder, cloud and mobile access, and integrations with other apps.

Suggested next features, in order

  1. Products and services list with inventory (A28–A32)
  2. Quotes / estimates and purchase orders (A1, A21)
  3. Bank statement import with matching (A38–A40)

  5. Attachments on transactions (A51)
  6. Classes and projects (A44–A46)
  7. Budgets and budget vs actual (A47)
“QuickBooks” here means QuickBooks Online (Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, Advanced) and QuickBooks Desktop (Pro, Premier, Enterprise). Some QuickBooks features need a higher plan or a paid add-on.
