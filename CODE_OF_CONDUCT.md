# Code of Conduct

TallyCheck is built for small businesses and the people who keep their books. We want everyone
who uses, discusses or contributes to it to feel welcome, whatever their experience, country,
language, background or identity.

This code applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other place where
people take part in the project, and when someone represents the project in public.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are accountants or business owners, not developers, and
  many write in their second or third language.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person. Say what you would change and why.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.
- Respect privacy. Never post real company, customer, employee or payroll data; a made-up
  company is enough to show a problem.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, intimidation, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address, email or financial
  details, without their permission.
- Repeated off-topic posting, spam or advertising.
- Any other behaviour a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, email **info@tallycheck.io** with
"Code of conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by the maintainers at Cenango (Pvt) Ltd and
kept confidential; we will reply within 5 working days. If a maintainer is involved, say so and
the report will be handled by the others.

Security problems are not reported here: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What happens next

The maintainers decide what action is fair, from a private reminder to a temporary or permanent
ban from the project's spaces. They may remove comments, commits, code or issues that break this
code. Decisions are explained to the people involved.
